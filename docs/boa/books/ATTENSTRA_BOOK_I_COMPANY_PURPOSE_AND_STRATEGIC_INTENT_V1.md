# Attenstra Book I Company Purpose and Strategic Intent V1

## Purpose

Define the enduring company-level purpose, origin logic, and strategic intent
of Attenstra.

## Current Baseline

This book should eventually answer:

- why Attenstra exists
- what enduring problem the company is built to address
- why the problem matters now
- what kind of company Attenstra intends to become

## Seed Context

The current strategic seed ideas already present in repo materials include:

- serious work is increasingly damaged by fragmented attention
- existing software is often mismatched to how important work actually unfolds
- Attenstra exists to create a new environment for serious work
