Privacy

Privacy notice

Attenstra.ai currently uses this website to share company and product direction and to collect email addresses from people who want updates.

What we collect

For the update list, we currently collect the email address you provide and a minimal source tag indicating that you subscribed via the Attenstra.ai website.

Why we collect it

We use this information to send occasional updates about Attenstra, PACE, and related product developments.

How subscriptions are handled

The production subscription flow is intended to run through Buttondown. If Buttondown is not yet configured, the site may fall back to opening a pre-filled email request instead of a direct form submission.

Your choice

You should only subscribe if you want to receive these updates. You can unsubscribe later through the email provider's unsubscribe flow or by contacting us directly.
