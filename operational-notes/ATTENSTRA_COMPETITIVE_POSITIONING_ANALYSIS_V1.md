Record a current competitive positioning analysis for Attenstra / PACE relative
to Claude Cowork, OpenClaw, and Microsoft Copilot.

## Competitive Set

### Claude Cowork

Official positioning:

- an AI coworker for knowledge work
- goal-oriented delegated execution across desktop, files, and apps
- currently presented by Anthropic as a research-preview product

Interpretation:

- Claude Cowork is easy to understand because it maps directly to a delegated
  task model: give it work, let it act, receive a deliverable.
- Its frame is narrower than PACE because it centers execution more than
  continuity, governance, and durable work progression across time.

### Microsoft Copilot

Official positioning:

- enterprise AI embedded across Microsoft 365 and related work surfaces
- an AI layer with agents, enterprise trust, and integration across the
  Microsoft stack

Interpretation:

- Microsoft Copilot is strongest where buyers want AI inside familiar existing
  software.
- It is powerful as an incumbent augmentation layer, but it is generally framed
  as AI within existing work surfaces rather than a new governed environment for
  serious work.

### OpenClaw

Official positioning:

- a personal AI assistant that actually does things
- open-source, flexible, tool-connected, and highly hackable

Interpretation:

- OpenClaw is strongest with power users, builders, and early adopters who want
  an assistant that can act across their personal systems.
- It is not naturally positioned for non-technical business buyers seeking a
  trusted environment for important cross-cutting work.

## Core Competitive Position

Attenstra / PACE should not be positioned as:

- another assistant
- another copilot
- another AI coworker
- a personal AI automation layer

Attenstra / PACE should be positioned as:

- the environment for serious work
- a governed environment for important work in a world of fragmented attention
- software that keeps what matters moving between moments of focus

## Comparative Wedge

The central wedge is:

- most competing systems help users do work with AI
- PACE is about creating the environment in which important work survives,
  compounds, and gets finished

This means PACE competes primarily on:

- continuity under fragmentation
- forward progress between attention intervals
- better use of human judgment
- preserving meaning, state, and momentum over time

It should not compete primarily on:

- model capability
- prompt quality
- generic time savings
- assistant novelty

## Competitor-Specific Positioning

### Against Claude Cowork

Use:

- Claude Cowork helps complete tasks.
- PACE is built for work that unfolds across time, interruption, and ongoing
  context.
- PACE is about continuity and finished outcomes, not just delegated execution.

Avoid:

- feature-comparison language
- model-comparison language
- claims that PACE is simply a more powerful coworker

### Against Microsoft Copilot

Use:

- Copilot brings AI into existing software.
- PACE is about reshaping the environment around the work itself.
- PACE matters when important work does not fit neatly into static existing
  surfaces.

Avoid:

- anti-Microsoft rhetoric
- generic governance claims without tying them to the work problem

### Against OpenClaw

Use:

- OpenClaw is a powerful personal assistant.
- PACE is a governed environment for serious work.
- PACE is designed for business-facing continuity, judgment, and outcomes.

Avoid:

- dismissing open-source positioning
- describing PACE as "enterprise OpenClaw"

## Recommended External Language

Recommended:

- The environment for serious work
- Built for a world of fragmented attention
- Keeps what matters moving
- Brings people back at the point of decision, not reconstruction

Avoid:

- assistant
- copilot
- coworker
- personal AI assistant
- generic productivity language like "work faster" or "save time"

## One-Line Competitive Position

Claude Cowork, OpenClaw, and Copilot help people use AI to do work.

PACE is about creating the governed environment in which important work
survives, compounds, and gets finished.

## Preferred Competitive Formulation

PACE is not another AI assistant or copilot. It is the environment where
serious work gets delivered in a world of fragmented attention.

## Summary

The most important strategic rule is:

- Attenstra / PACE must be marketed as a new environment for serious work, not
  as another AI helper inside fragmented existing tooling.
